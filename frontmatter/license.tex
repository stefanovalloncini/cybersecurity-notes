\section*{How to give credit}

\begin{quote}
\notesauthor, \emph{\notestitle}, course notes, 2026. \notesurl[] Licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
\end{quote}

If you changed the notes, add \enquote{adapted by \textit{your name}} and say what you changed.
